**Original browser:** Search by Drice Roland (@driceroland), Office Commun, and the contributors to https://github.com/driceroland/Search. The browser and browsing engine integration are their work. Search’s MIT copyright remains Copyright (c) 2026 Office Commun.

**Folder port and package:** Noah Helms (@haon-v2). This module ports the behavior of Noah’s original Curve browser sidebar folders: its TabFolder model, folder actions, import/export format, folder-scoped curved tabs, and hover guides. The SwiftUI implementation lives in the separate Search Appearance Mod Loader repository; it does not embed the old Curve browser or create additional browsing views.

Curve’s dotted hook guide was visually inspired by RareUI’s HookSidebar. The native drawing here is independently implemented; no RareUI component code is bundled.
